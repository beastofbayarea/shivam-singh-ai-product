# Financial-Services Agent Platform — Governed Production Workflows

> **Portfolio lens:** Agentic platform strategy, governed execution, RAG infrastructure, model routing, and enterprise economics.

## Executive snapshot

Defined a managed execution layer that moved financial-services agents from prototypes into production. The product combined managed reasoning, integrated knowledge, permissioned actions, configurable traces, circuit breakers, human approval, and cost-aware model routing.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led product strategy for a governed agent platform that reduced deployment time 40% and compressed selected pilots from eight weeks to three days.
- Prioritized an integrated knowledge layer that reduced RAG setup from days to under five minutes while preserving controlled access to private enterprise data.
- Introduced traceability, execution limits, approval checkpoints, and model routing that lowered routine-query cost up to 50% and generated roughly $12M in annualized savings.

## Interview story

### Situation

Customers could demonstrate agent intelligence but stalled on orchestration, vector infrastructure, permissions, auditability, runaway execution, and unit economics. Missing traceability alone could add six to eight weeks to compliance review.

### Task

Turn a generic conversational capability into a secure, observable, bounded, and economically sustainable workflow platform.

### Actions

- Mapped production blockers to explicit platform capabilities rather than adding more model features.
- Selected managed retrieval as the default journey, prioritizing time-to-first-grounded-agent over infrastructure choice.
- Made trace depth configurable by environment and risk, accepting targeted latency overhead where auditability created greater value.
- Classified work by complexity and risk so routine requests used lower-cost models and high-stakes work used advanced reasoning plus human checkpoints.

### Results

- Deployment time improved 40%, with selected pilots moving from eight weeks to three days.
- Knowledge-base setup fell below five minutes.
- Routine-query costs fell by as much as 50%, supporting roughly $12M in annualized savings.
- Beachhead workflows reduced legacy-modernization effort nearly 80%, made mortgage-agent users three times more likely to close, and saved more than 250,000 support hours annually.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Bundle a managed knowledge layer while retaining controlled flexibility for advanced teams.
- Accept configurable tracing overhead to remove a much larger compliance delay.
- Measure production workflows and business outcomes rather than prototype count.

## Leadership signal

Aligned platform engineering, risk, compliance, application teams, and financial-services customers around a single product thesis: safe action—not better chat—was the path to production.
