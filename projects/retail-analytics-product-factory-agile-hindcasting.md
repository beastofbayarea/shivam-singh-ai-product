>
> **Timeline alignment:** The [public AI Product Management resume](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-ai-product/blob/main/shivam-singh-ai-product.pdf) is used only to place this case within the McKinsey role dated 2014-2016.

## Evidence-grounded premise

The Agile Manifesto prioritizes customer collaboration, working increments, and responsiveness to change. Tashman's forecasting research supports rolling-origin, multiple-period out-of-sample evaluation. McKinsey's 2011 big-data research identified retail value but stressed workflow, talent, incentives, and access to data. These sources support a cross-functional analytics product factory with historical validation gates.

## Case approach

- Organize business owners, analysts, engineers, and implementers around a prioritized decision backlog.
- Deliver small working increments and review them with decision users.
- Hindcast each model across multiple historical periods and expose assumptions, error, and exceptions.
- Release only when analytical validity and workflow adoption criteria are both met.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use cycle time, out-of-sample error, decision adoption, override reasons, reproducibility, and realized decision quality. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
