# Veeqo — Merchant Assurance & Shipping Platform Strategy

> **Portfolio lens:** Product strategy, platform economics, merchant workflows, GenAI data translation, and outcome-led retention.

## Executive snapshot

Repositioned shipping software from a commoditized label tool into a merchant operating system. Free core software became the acquisition layer, while shipping volume, rebates, routing, warehouse accuracy, delivery compliance, and product-data intelligence created recurring value.

## Resume-ready impact

- Shifted the product from paid label software to merchant assurance, aligning free adoption with shipping-volume economics and outcome-based retention.
- Reduced late deliveries 20% and achieved 99.89% picking accuracy in a cited merchant deployment through delivery-risk controls and guided warehouse scanning.
- Delivered up to $100K in annual routing savings for one seller and embedded GenAI-assisted catalog mapping across multiple commerce channels.

## Interview story

### Situation

Competitors could copy label features and compete on price, while merchants lost more value through warehouse mistakes, inefficient routing, inconsistent listings, and delivery-policy failures.

### Task

Design a durable platform and business model around merchant outcomes rather than subscription access to commodity functionality.

### Actions

- Mapped the full order journey from listing through delivery and placed controls at each margin or compliance leak.
- Made core software free and tied economics to payment and shipping activity, credits, and retained volume.
- Introduced smart routing, warehouse scanning, and delivery-risk prediction with explicit intervention thresholds.
- Created a canonical product record and GenAI-assisted attribute mapping with exception review for channel-specific taxonomies.

### Results

- Late deliveries declined 20%.
- One merchant reached 99.89% picking accuracy.
- A cited seller saved roughly $100K annually through routing.
- The product expanded from labels into listing, picking, routing, shipping, compliance, and learning loops.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Use free software as an acquisition layer, not the entire economic model.
- Prioritize continued marketplace eligibility and delivery outcomes over feature parity.
- Keep confidence thresholds and human review around AI-generated attribute mappings.

## Leadership signal

Connected merchant discovery, commercial-model design, data science, warehouse operations, policy teams, and carrier economics into one product strategy.
